# Star Harvester v2.1

**▶ [Play Star Harvester in your browser](https://vertith.github.io/Star-Harvester-V2.1/)**

A strategy card game for 1–4 players, created by Adam Smothers. Rival mining corporations race to build an empire in the asteroid belt: harvest chips, scan and build blueprints, grow each building along its upgrade tree, and knock rivals back when they get close.

## Play

Play online at **https://vertith.github.io/Star-Harvester-V2.1/** (desktop or phone), or download and open `index.html` in any modern web browser. Everything (art, music and rules) is inside that one file; no install or internet connection is needed, apart from the web fonts.

- Play against up to three bots on Normal, Hard, Expert or Insane, or hot-seat with friends on one screen.
- New to the belt? Start the 5-turn training flight from the main screen.
- Earn 14 achievements, shown on the main screen.

**Win:** end your turn with your Base at Level 6 and five buildings at Level 4.
